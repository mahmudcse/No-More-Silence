# Terms of Service (Draft)

> **Status:** Draft for Legal Review (Bangladeshi Legal Context)  
> **Applicable Regulations:** Cyber Security Act / Cyber Security Ordinance, Defamation Law (Penal Code § 499/500), Nari o Shishu Nirjatan Daman Ain § 14 (Victim Identity Protection).

---

## 1. Nature of the Registry

1.1. **No More Silence** is a public-interest documentation registry and civil-society research archive. Its objective is to record, verify, and archive publicly available statements and comments involving sexual violence threats, harassment, victim-blaming, and rape apologia made on publicly accessible digital platforms.

1.2. The platform operates on a strict evidentiary basis:
- The registry documents **only verified, verbatim digital records** of public statements.
- Records are classified neutrally as **"recorded comment"** and **"verified record"**. The platform does not make judicial determinations of criminal guilt or assign personal defamatory labels.

---

## 2. Reporting & Submissions

2.1. Submissions are made voluntarily by members of the public ("Reporters") either anonymously or through registered accounts.

2.2. By submitting evidence (screenshots, URLs, metadata), the Reporter certifies that:
- The material reflects a genuine, unmanipulated public comment or post.
- The submission is made in good faith without intent to extort, harass, frame, or defame any individual.
- Fabricating digital evidence, manipulating screenshots, or submitting malicious false reports is strictly prohibited. Submissions identified as fabricated will be permanently rejected, and associated accounts or IP hashes blacklisted.

---

## 3. Strict Victim Protection (Zero Victim PII Policy)

3.1. In strict compliance with Section 14 of the Nari o Shishu Nirjatan Daman Ain and ethical survivor-protection norms, **the identity, name, photograph, address, educational institution, family details, or identifiable information of any victim of sexual violence is never stored or published on this platform**.

3.2. All evidence uploaded to the platform undergo mandatory redaction to obscure any third-party or victim details prior to any public disclosure.

---

## 4. Rights of Profile Owners (Appeals & Right of Reply)

4.1. Any individual associated with a public Facebook profile recorded on this registry holds the right to submit a formal Appeal or Right of Reply.

4.2. **Grounds for Appeal:**
- Impersonation or fake profile creation by a third party.
- Account hijacking / compromised account at the time the comment was posted.
- Verifiable proof that the screenshot/evidence was manipulated or fabricated.
- Name/URL collision or inaccurate profile attribution.

4.3. **Right of Reply:**
- Profile owners who acknowledge the comment may submit a formal statement, apology, or clarification. If accepted by the appeals review board, this response will be displayed directly alongside the public record.

4.4. **Takedown SLA:**
- Validated appeals involving misidentification, account compromise, or fabricated evidence will result in the immediate retraction and unpublishing of the record within 24 hours of verification.

---

## 5. Limitation of Liability

5.1. Content displayed on the platform reflects copies and transcriptions of public comments made by third parties on external social networks. No More Silence does not author third-party comments and disclaims all liability for the speech of third-party commenters.

5.2. Information is provided "as is" for public awareness, research, and documentation purposes. The platform does not advocate vigilantism, harassment, doxxing, or extra-legal retaliatory actions against any individual recorded in the registry.
